Computer Science @ Carnegie Mellon University, Class of 2030

**Projects**

* [flood-classification-hnm](https://github.com/zinnialily/flood-classification-hnm) (Research Intern, CRIS-HAZARD, University of South Florida)
  * **Problem:** Flood-detection AI kept mistaking river water for flooding.
  * **Solution:** Built a model that caught 99% of real floods, then tested a common fix for the river-water mistakes and showed it didn't beat a simple free baseline.

* [bridgesdr](https://github.com/zinnialily/bridgesdr) (Non-Trivial Fellowship, top ~2% of 7,000 applicants)
  * **Problem:** AI-generated disaster images might work worse for some parts of the world than others.
  * **Solution:** Ran the first study comparing them across regions and posted the paper on SSRN (90+ downloads).

* [capstone_project_bwsi](https://github.com/zinnialily/capstone_project_bwsi) (MIT Beaver Works Summer Institute)
  * **Problem:** After a hurricane, damaged roads make it hard to get supplies where they're needed.
  * **Solution:** Built a program that plans delivery routes for 150,000 supplies across 5 distribution centers and 40 trucks.

* [rescue](https://github.com/zinnialily/rescue) (Independent Research, presented to the Fort Bend Chamber of Commerce)
  * **Problem:** Disasters block roads and overload hospitals, so patients wait longer for help.
  * **Solution:** Built a program that plans ambulance routes around road damage and hospital space, tested four methods across 15 cities, and found the best one reached every victim 14.5% faster than a basic approach.
